# Speed Styles skill

Use your AI tool to open the brand concept from your Speed Styles quiz. Ask it to change a colour, try a different headline or update your business card. Your edits stay in your folder until you approve them.

## Set me up

In Claude Code, the **Code** tab of Claude Desktop with a local project, Codex, or Cursor, paste this line into a chat with access to a local folder.

> Install the speedstyle skill from https://github.com/sticktrk/brand-design-skills in my AI tool, then set me up for Speed Styles.

Your tool opens a sign-in window on your computer. Use the address you gave in the quiz and the six-digit code from your email. You can sign in while your concept is being made.

If your tool asks for an install command, use the following line. The installer needs Node.js 22.20 or later. Pick the AI tool you use when prompted. You can also ask your tool to copy the `skills/speedstyle` folder into its personal skills folder and run its helper with Node.js 22 or later.

```sh
npx skills add sticktrk/brand-design-skills --skill speedstyle -g
```

## Open and edit your concept

When your ready email arrives, tell your AI tool **“Open my Speed Styles concept.”** You can also paste the viewing link. The first download needs an internet connection. Keep the folder to return to your edits. Delete it to discard them.

Try **“Make the green a little darker everywhere”** or **“Change the tagline to ‘Flowers delivered by bike’.”** Refresh the preview to see the result. For new photography or logo artwork, ask your tool to send your request to the Speed Styles team.

## Approve and start your trial

Say **“I’m happy with this. Approve it.”** Your tool shows you the changes and asks you to confirm. Approval sends the files you reviewed and starts work on your full brand kit.

We’ll email you when your brand kit is ready. Your 14-day trial starts at delivery. The email includes your kit and the link to keep it. You can also ask **“What now?”** to find your brand guide.
